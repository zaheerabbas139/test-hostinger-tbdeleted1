Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, direct answers regarding our Microsoft enterprise services, cloud migration strategies, cyber security standards, contracts, and 24/7 SLA commitments.

General What services does Prana Solutions Tech offer?

We offer comprehensive enterprise IT services including 24/7 Managed IT Support, Cloud Architecture & Migration (Microsoft Azure, Microsoft 365), Cyber Security & Zero Trust hardening, and Digital Transformation using Microsoft Dynamics 365, Power Platform, and proprietary tooling such as BridgeAD.

General Do you support businesses of all sizes?

Yes. We partner with small and mid-market organizations through fully outsourced IT operations, as well as large enterprise and public-sector institutions requiring specialized cloud migration engineering, security compliance, or co-managed tier-3 escalation architecture.

Cloud Solutions Can you help migrate our on-premises infrastructure to the cloud?

Yes. We specialize in end-to-end cloud migrations into Microsoft Azure and hybrid architectures. Our solutions architects baseline your physical and virtual environments, execute non-disruptive pre-flight dry runs, design landing zones aligned with the Microsoft Cloud Adoption Framework (CAF), and orchestrate cutovers with minimal or zero downtime.

Cloud Solutions What are the business and operational benefits of moving to Microsoft 365?

Microsoft 365 provides enterprise-grade productivity tools, centralized identity via Microsoft Entra ID, unified endpoint management with Microsoft Intune, automated compliance policies, native threat protection via Microsoft Defender, anywhere collaboration, and drastically lower on-premises server licensing and maintenance overhead.

Pricing & Contracts How is pricing structured for your services?

We offer transparent, predictable pricing models tailored to your requirements: fixed monthly per-user/per-device pricing for Managed Services with guaranteed SLAs; milestone-based fixed-fee statements of work (SOW) for cloud migrations and implementations; and flexible architectural consulting retainers.

Pricing & Contracts What is the typical contract length for managed IT services?

Our standard managed services agreements are 12 months with quarterly business reviews (QBRs) and predictable monthly invoicing. We also offer multi-year agreements with price protection, as well as short-term project statements of work for specific migration or audit engagements.

Managed IT Services What is included in your Managed IT Services agreements?

Our managed services include proactive 24/7/365 infrastructure monitoring, unlimited helpdesk remote user support, automated patch management for OS and third-party software, cloud backup and disaster recovery validation, endpoint antivirus and EDR management, and proactive vendor escalation management.

Managed IT Services What is your guaranteed response time for technical support requests?

We guarantee an initial response time of under 15 minutes for critical Priority 1 (business-down) incidents, and under 2 hours for standard service requests. Our Network Operations Center (NOC) and Service Desk operate 24/7/365 with automated escalation pathways.

Security How do you protect our organization against cyber threats and ransomware?

We deploy a multi-layered Zero Trust defense model utilizing the Microsoft Security Stack: Microsoft Defender for Endpoint/Office 365, Microsoft Sentinel SIEM/SOAR, Microsoft Entra Conditional Access with mandatory MFA, automated phishing simulations, privileged access management, and round-the-clock threat telemetry monitoring.

Security Do you provide regulatory compliance and security audit support?

Yes. We actively guide organizations through certifications and regulatory standards including ISO 27001, Cyber Essentials / Cyber Essentials Plus, GDPR, HIPAA, and SOC 2. We perform gap assessments, implement compensating technical controls, and provide immutable audit evidence for certifying bodies.
