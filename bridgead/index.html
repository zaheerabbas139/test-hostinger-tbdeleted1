Enterprise Trust

Migration Content is Streamed, Not Retained at Rest

BridgeAD persists only the operational metadata needed to orchestrate and audit execution. Mail, files, credentials, and message bodies are never written to disk in BridgeAD infrastructure.

  • ✓End-to-End Encryption: All Microsoft Graph, Exchange, and control plane traffic enforces TLS 1.2+ with mutual authentication.
  • ✓Azure Key Vault & DPAPI: Secrets remain encrypted; access tokens are ephemeral and never logged.
  • ✓Five-Tier RBAC & Mandatory MFA: Granular roles (Viewer to Super Admin) with full audit traceability on all operations.
  • ✓Self-Hosted Delivery: Deploy the control plane directly in your Azure subscription or Kubernetes cluster for zero egress.

Technical Answers

Frequently Asked Questions About BridgeAD

Detailed technical answers covering architecture, workload readiness, and security boundaries.

Cloud SolutionsDo we need an agent on every user workstation?

Not for directory or Microsoft 365 workload migrations: one healthy agent per reachable domain is often sufficient. Device-local workflows such as cloud-native conversion require a healthy agent running as SYSTEM on each target Windows device for the conversion window.

SecurityDoes BridgeAD store our mailbox, file, or chat content?

No. All customer migration content is streamed directly in transit from source to destination. Only operational telemetry and metadata required to orchestrate work (job state, error counts, audit correlation IDs) are persisted – never bodies of mail, files, or messages.

Cloud SolutionsWhat is your Microsoft 365 workload migration readiness?

Exchange Online mailbox content (including certified any-to-any routes across Gmail, IMAP, on-premises Exchange, and PST/EML archives), SharePoint and OneDrive content, and Teams structure reconstruction are supported. Teams channel-message import is conditional on Microsoft protected-API approval.

SecurityWhere does our data live in the SaaS edition?

You pin a primary Azure region during provisioning. All customer-scoped data, secrets in Azure Key Vault, and audit logs remain strictly within that region under sovereign data residency guarantees.

Managed IT ServicesCan we run BridgeAD on-prem or inside our own Azure subscription?

Yes. The self-hosted edition deploys via Helm chart, raw Kubernetes manifests, or Docker Compose, and runs entirely inside your subscription or private datacenter. Both SaaS and self-hosted editions ship from the exact same codebase.

SecurityHow is administrative access and privilege controlled?

BridgeAD enforces a strict five-tier Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) model: Viewer, Migration Operator, Tenant Admin, Platform Admin, and Super Admin. MFA is mandatory for every privileged role, and all privilege changes write to an immutable audit ledger.

Pricing & ContractsCan BridgeAD integrate with our enterprise ITSM and SIEM pipelines?

Yes. BridgeAD provides authenticated REST APIs and signed webhook notifications for real-time job events, audit automation, and integration with ITSM platforms (ServiceNow, Jira Service Management) and SIEM solutions (Microsoft Sentinel, Splunk).

Pricing & ContractsDo you offer a sandbox or proof-of-concept (PoC)?

Yes. We offer fully scoped technical proofs-of-concept. Contact our solutions architects with your source and destination tenant parameters to schedule a sandbox assessment.
